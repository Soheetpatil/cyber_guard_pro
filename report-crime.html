Cybercrime Reporting & Response

Comprehensive guide for reporting cyber incidents and coordinating effective response strategies

🚨 Emergency Response Protocol

For immediate threats to life, safety, or critical infrastructure, contact emergency services (911 in the US) immediately. For active cyber incidents affecting your organization, initiate your incident response plan and contact your security team.

Official Reporting Agencies

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

Primary Focus: Federal cybercrime investigation and coordination

Website: ic3.gov

Phone: 1-800-CALL-FBI (1-800-225-5324)

Report These Incidents:

  • Business Email Compromise (BEC)
  • Ransomware attacks
  • Online fraud and scams
  • Identity theft
  • Cryptocurrency fraud
  • Intellectual property theft
Response Time: 24-48 hours for acknowledgment

CISA Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency

Primary Focus: Critical infrastructure protection and coordination

Website: cisa.gov/report

Phone: 1-888-282-0870

Report These Incidents:

  • Critical infrastructure attacks
  • Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)
  • Supply chain compromises
  • Nation-state attacks
  • Zero-day exploits
  • Significant data breaches
Response Time: Immediate for critical infrastructure

Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

Primary Focus: Consumer protection and fraud prevention

Website: reportfraud.ftc.gov

Phone: 1-877-FTC-HELP (1-877-382-4357)

Report These Incidents:

  • Consumer fraud and scams
  • Identity theft
  • Online shopping fraud
  • Tech support scams
  • Romance scams
  • Investment fraud
Response Time: Automated confirmation, investigation varies

Local Law Enforcement

Primary Focus: Local cybercrime investigation and immediate response

Contact: Your local police department or sheriff's office

When to Contact: Immediate threats or local impact incidents

Report These Incidents:

  • Cyberstalking and harassment
  • Threats of violence
  • Sextortion and exploitation
  • Local business fraud
  • Computer intrusion with local impact
  • Immediate physical threats
Response Time: Immediate for emergencies

Incident Response Framework

1

Preparation

  • Establish incident response team
  • Develop response procedures
  • Implement monitoring systems
  • Conduct regular training exercises
  • Maintain updated contact lists
2

Identification

  • Detect and analyze security events
  • Determine if incident occurred
  • Document initial findings
  • Assess potential impact
  • Classify incident severity
3

Containment

  • Isolate affected systems
  • Prevent further damage
  • Preserve evidence
  • Implement short-term fixes
  • Maintain business operations
4

Eradication

  • Remove malware and threats
  • Close attack vectors
  • Patch vulnerabilities
  • Update security controls
  • Strengthen defenses
5

Recovery

  • Restore systems from clean backups
  • Implement additional monitoring
  • Gradually return to normal operations
  • Validate system integrity
  • Monitor for recurring issues
6

Lessons Learned

  • Conduct post-incident review
  • Document lessons learned
  • Update response procedures
  • Improve security controls
  • Share intelligence with community

Effective Reporting Best Practices

📋

Document Everything

  • Maintain detailed incident logs
  • Capture screenshots and evidence
  • Record timestamps and sequences
  • Preserve system logs and artifacts
  • Document all response actions
⚡

Act Quickly

  • Report incidents within 24 hours
  • Implement immediate containment
  • Notify stakeholders promptly
  • Preserve evidence before it's lost
  • Coordinate with legal and compliance
🎯

Provide Complete Information

  • Include all relevant technical details
  • Specify affected systems and data
  • Estimate impact and scope
  • Provide accurate contact information
  • Share indicators of compromise
🔄

Follow Up Consistently

  • Track case numbers and references
  • Provide updates as investigation progresses
  • Respond to agency requests promptly
  • Maintain communication channels
  • Document final resolution

Common Incident Types & Response Procedures

🔒

Ransomware Attack

Critical

Immediate Actions (First 30 minutes)

  1. Isolate affected systems from the network immediately
  2. Do NOT shut down infected systems (preserve memory evidence)
  3. Document all visible ransom messages and file extensions
  4. Contact law enforcement and your cyber insurance provider
  5. Activate your incident response team
  6. Begin inventory of affected systems and data

Evidence Preservation

  • Take photos of ransom messages on screens
  • Preserve system logs and network traffic captures
  • Document timeline of events and user activities
  • Collect samples of encrypted files

Recovery Process

  • Assess backup integrity and availability
  • Identify and patch the initial attack vector
  • Rebuild systems from clean backups or images
  • Implement additional monitoring and controls
📧

Business Email Compromise (BEC)

High

Immediate Actions (First 15 minutes)

  1. Secure the compromised email account immediately
  2. Change passwords and revoke all active sessions
  3. Review recent sent items and email rules
  4. Check for unauthorized forwarding rules
  5. Notify financial institutions if wire transfers involved
  6. Alert all employees about potential impersonation

Investigation Process

  • Analyze email headers and authentication records
  • Review login logs and IP addresses
  • Examine mailbox permissions and delegates
  • Check for data exfiltration or unauthorized access
💳

Data Breach

Critical

Immediate Actions (First 60 minutes)

  1. Contain the breach and prevent further data loss
  2. Assess the scope and type of data compromised
  3. Document all evidence and maintain chain of custody
  4. Notify legal counsel and compliance team
  5. Begin regulatory notification process (GDPR: 72 hours)
  6. Prepare customer and stakeholder communications

Regulatory Requirements

GDPR 72 hours to authorities
CCPA Without unreasonable delay
HIPAA 60 days to individuals
PCI DSS Immediately to card brands
